# Practice question

Explore the employee directory, change an employee, and inspect the details and history views.

Add support for scheduling employee changes for a future date.

A salary change entered on May 3 but effective May 10 should appear immediately in the employee’s history. The details page should show the current salary until May 10, then show the new salary.

Modify the frontend enough to make the workflow functional. Frontend polish is not required. Verify your solution with tests and be prepared to explain your engineering decisions, alternatives, and tradeoffs.
